What should I wear?+

For Haldi, bring your brightest yellow ethnic outfit. For Sangeet, wear Indo-Western attire. For the wedding, dress in your royal best—your favourite festive outfit and finest look.

Are all the celebrations at one venue?+

Yes. Haldi, Sangeet, and the wedding will be at Blue Mountain Country Club & Resort, Siliguri. Use “Get directions” above to find the venue.

When do the celebrations begin?+

Haldi: 25 January 2027 at 10:00 AM. Sangeet: 25 January 2027 at 6:00 PM. Wedding: 26 January 2027 at 7:00 PM. All times are India Standard Time (IST).

How do I send my RSVP?+

Use the RSVP section above, review your reply, then tap “Send via WhatsApp” to open the couple’s chat.
